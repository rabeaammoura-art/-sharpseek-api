# Backlit train plaque, PETG (slice with the 0.24mm Standard profile: faster)

Slots of the plaque 3MF: 1 PETG Blue (opaque), 2 AD-PETG translucent white, 3 translucent yellow, 4 translucent orange-red (bear).
Tray = AD-PETG opaque white (print back panel down; LED strips stick on its three back bars, USB-C slot in the bottom wall).
Base = one long base, any PETG. Estimates: H2D, 0.24 mm Standard, prime tower, +-20 %. Nothing print-tested (printer not delivered yet).

| size | plaque | tray | base | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 280 mm | 4h14m, 85 g | 1h40m, 50 g | 0h40m, 19 g | about 6h34m, 154 g, 1.1 JOD |
| 200 mm | 2h38m, 45 g | 1h09m, 35 g | 0h35m, 15 g | about 4h22m, 96 g, 0.71 JOD |

The base is 60 % of the plaque width (one long piece with the tilted slot). Arch windows under it were tried: -4 g but +7 min, so not used.

The base is a railway track in ONE colour, printed flat: two long rails and six evenly spaced cross sleepers fused with them (a ladder; the sleepers reach behind the rear rail as a tail so it does not tip back; none at the centre for the USB-C cable).
Locking (no glue): on each rail, at the two lock positions, there is a hook shaped like an upside-down L: a stem and a horizontal pin with a small head.
The two FRONT hooks go through L-shaped keyhole slots in the plaque's tongues; the two REAR hooks go through the same slots in two back tabs of the tray. The tray and plaque hang on the four pins, leaning back 12 degrees.
Assembly: slide the plaque into the tray from the front; lower both onto the hooks (the heads pass the wide legs of the slots), then slide them 8 mm to the LEFT: the pins sit in the narrow legs and the heads hold both parts.
Software check: no interference when entering, halfway or locked; a lift, a pull or a push of 1.5-2 mm collides (locked). Not print-tested.

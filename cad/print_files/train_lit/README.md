# Backlit train plaque, PETG (slice with the 0.24mm Standard profile: faster)

Slots of the plaque 3MF: 1 PETG Blue (opaque), 2 AD-PETG translucent white, 3 translucent yellow, 4 translucent orange-red (bear).
Tray = AD-PETG opaque white (print back panel down; LED strips stick on its three back bars, USB-C slot in the bottom wall).
Base = one long base, any PETG. Estimates: H2D, 0.24 mm Standard, prime tower, +-20 %. Nothing print-tested (printer not delivered yet).

| size | plaque | tray | base | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 280 mm | 4h14m, 85 g | 1h32m, 48 g | 0h44m, 22 g | about 6h36m, 156 g, 1.2 JOD |
| 200 mm | 2h38m, 45 g | 1h07m, 35 g | 0h37m, 18 g | about 4h23m, 98 g, 0.77 JOD |

The base is 60 % of the plaque width (one long piece with the tilted slot). Arch windows under it were tried: -4 g but +7 min, so not used.

The base is a railway track in ONE colour, printed flat: two long rails and six evenly spaced cross sleepers fused with them (a ladder, none at the centre for the USB-C cable).
Locking (no glue), on the two sleepers at +-key_x: (1) the tray's lowest wall has two holes; two 6 mm pegs on the slot floor go into them (holds the tray),
(2) the plaque has two 12 mm tongues at its bottom that pass through two cut-outs in the tray's bottom wall into two pockets in the base (holds the plaque).
Assembly: slide the plaque into the tray from the front, then lower the tray with the plaque into the base slot. Fit checked in software (interference < 3 mm3), not print-tested.

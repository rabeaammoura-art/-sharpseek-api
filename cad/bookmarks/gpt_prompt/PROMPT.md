# Cut-out bookmark - GPT image prompts (3D ZONE TECH)

Attach `1_blueprint.png` then the style reference picture (kept out of git), then paste one prompt.
The result must be a flat black/white stencil: black = printed, white = cut through. It is then converted to STL in this repo.

## rose_arch

```
Create ONE flat, perfectly front-on, pure BLACK-and-WHITE stencil drawing of a tall bookmark.
Canvas 1024 x 3072 px (portrait, about 1:3), plain white background, the bookmark centred and filling about 94% of the canvas height.
No perspective, no rotation, no shadow, no 3D, no texture, no grey, no colour, no tassel, no cord, no book, no text, no signature.

I attached 2 images:
- Image 1 is the exact BLUEPRINT (45 x 165 mm bookmark). Copy its outer outline, the solid black frame (2.6 mm wide),
  the solid black top band and the round white tassel hole EXACTLY, same proportions and positions.
  The light-grey area is where you draw the design; in your drawing that area becomes white with the black design in it.
- Image 2 is the STYLE reference (a laser-cut paper bookmark). Match its style, density and elegance,
  but draw a new original design; do not trace it.

DESIGN (pink bookmark in image 2): a single climbing ROSE. One large open rose bloom in the upper third, drawn with
overlapping petal shapes separated by thin cut lines; one curved stem rising from the bottom; 6-8 rose leaves with a centre
vein, in pairs along the stem, a few small buds. At the very top of the grey area a small gothic ARCH window tracery
(pointed arches and curls); in the two bottom corners a small diagonal LATTICE (trellis) that the stem grows out of.

MEANING OF THE COLOURS: BLACK = solid material that will be 3D printed. WHITE = empty hole cut through.
Only these two colours, hard crisp edges, no anti-aliased grey areas, no outlines-only drawing: every stem, leaf and
petal is a FILLED black shape.

PRINTING RULES (very important, the drawing becomes a 1 mm thick 3D-printed part):
1. ONE connected piece: every black shape must be attached to the black frame directly or through other black shapes.
   No floating black islands anywhere.
2. Many attachment points: the design must touch the frame at 12 or more places spread over both long sides,
   the top band and the bottom, so the stem is supported along its whole length.
3. Minimum black thickness: no black line or neck thinner than 1/45 of the bookmark width (about 23 px on this canvas).
   Stems about 1/25 of the width.
4. Minimum white gap: cut lines (leaf veins, petal separations) at least 1/70 of the width (about 15 px) and
   they must NOT cut a shape into separate pieces: every vein/petal cut stops before the edges of its shape.
5. No tiny details, no dots, no hair-thin tendrils, no hatching.
6. Keep the frame, the top band and the hole untouched; the design stays inside the grey area and merges into the frame.

Before you finish, check rules 1-4 again and fix anything that breaks them. Output only the final image.
```
